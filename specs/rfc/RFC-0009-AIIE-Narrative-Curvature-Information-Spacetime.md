## 1. Abstract

This RFC formalizes **Narrative Curvature** $\kappa$ as a measurable physical quantity within the AIIE framework. Unlike metaphorical interpretations of "information gravity," this theory defines curvature as the second-order temporal derivative of narrative distance $d(s_a, s_b)$ on a metric state space $\mathcal{M}$. By establishing the equation of motion $\ddot{d} = -\kappa \cdot d$, we provide a deterministic mapping of how massive geopolitical events distort the information spacetime, forcing the convergence or fragmentation of disparate narrative trajectories. This formulation establishes narrative dynamics as a second-order dynamical system on a metric state space, satisfying the formal structure of classical field theory.

## 2. Mathematical Definition of Curvature

### 2.1 Axiomatization of Narrative Distance $d(s_a, s_b)$

Narrative distance $d: \mathcal{M} \times \mathcal{M} \to \mathbb{R}_{\geq 0}$ is defined as a metric on the state space $\mathcal{M}$, satisfying:

1. $d(s_a, s_b) \geq 0$, with $d(s_a, s_b) = 0 \iff s_a = s_b$ *(non-negativity / identity)*
2. $d(s_a, s_b) = d(s_b, s_a)$ *(symmetry)*
3. $d(s_a, s_c) \leq d(s_a, s_b) + d(s_b, s_c)$ *(triangle inequality)*

In the AIIE implementation, this metric is realized via the cosine distance between 35TAG-derived state vectors:

$$d(s_a, s_b) = 1 - \frac{\langle \mathbf{v}_a, \mathbf{v}_b \rangle}{\|\mathbf{v}_a\| \cdot \|\mathbf{v}_b\|}$$

where $\mathbf{v}_a, \mathbf{v}_b \in \mathbb{R}^{35}$ are the 35TAG state vectors.

### 2.2 Narrative Curvature $\kappa$

Narrative curvature is defined as the second-order temporal derivative of the inter-narrative distance:

$$\kappa(t) = \frac{d^2}{dt^2} d(s_a(t), s_b(t))$$

**Physical interpretation:**

| $\kappa$ | Geometry | Narrative Dynamics |
|----------|----------|--------------------|
| $\kappa < 0$ | Positive curvature (convergent) | Narratives attracted toward consensus — "information gravity" |
| $\kappa = 0$ | Flat spacetime | Narratives evolve independently — no field interaction |
| $\kappa > 0$ | Negative curvature (divergent) | Narratives repel — Excited State (RFC-0006 F4 result) |

## 3. Equation of Motion

The narrative equation of motion on the curved metric state space:

$$\ddot{d}(t) = -\kappa(t) \cdot d(t)$$

This is structurally equivalent to a harmonic oscillator with a time-varying spring constant $\kappa(t)$.

**Boundary conditions:**

- **Collapse** ($\kappa \to -\infty$): $d(t) \to 0$ — narratives crystallize into a single world line (T28 collapse_state)
- **Fragmentation** ($\kappa \to +\infty$): $d(t) \to \infty$ — PCE ignition (RFC-0002)
- **Geodesic** ($\kappa = 0$): free propagation — least-action path (RFC-0008 PNLA)

## 4. Geodesic and Curvature Tensor

### 4.1 Narrative Geodesic

A narrative trajectory $\gamma(t)$ is a **geodesic** on $\mathcal{M}$ if it minimizes the path length:

$$L[\gamma] = \int_0^T \|\dot{\gamma}(t)\| \, dt$$

This is the geometric realization of the least-action principle (RFC-0008): the realized trajectory is the geodesic of the narrative field.

### 4.2 Discrete Curvature Estimation

From observed state sequences $\{s_0, s_1, \ldots, s_T\}$, curvature is estimated as:

$$\hat{\kappa}_t = \frac{d_{t+1} - 2d_t + d_{t-1}}{(\Delta t)^2}$$

where $d_t = d(s_t^{(a)}, s_t^{(b)})$ and $\Delta t = T06_t$.
